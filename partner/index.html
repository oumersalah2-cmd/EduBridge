EduBridge Partner Integration Showcase

Demonstrating 2-Minute Third-Party Installation into Any External Web Platform

How Any Partner Embeds EduBridge in Under 2 Minutes:

  1. Step 1: Copy the zero-dependency embed loader script below.
  2. Step 2: Paste it anywhere into your HTML page where you want the player or job card to appear.
  3. Step 3: The Web Component automatically mounts an isolated, accessible player with full keyboard, caption, and sign language controls!
<script src="../player/embed.js" data-src="../demo/data/lesson.mp4" data-captions="../demo/data/lesson.en.vtt,../demo/data/lesson.am.vtt" data-sign="../demo/data/lesson.ethsl.mp4" data-lang="am"></script>
